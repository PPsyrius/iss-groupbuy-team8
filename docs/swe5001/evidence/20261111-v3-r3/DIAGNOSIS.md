## Important on-demand rollout constraint

The pinned Terraform AWS provider 5.100.0 can force ECS service replacement on a capacity-provider weight-only edit unless `force_new_deployment=true` and both strategies remain nonempty. Therefore “set Spot weight to zero” is **not** an approved one-line apply. Any future proposal must prove in-place service updates in a private saved plan, deliberately roll at the normal floor before measurement, verify actual tasks are on demand and healthy, and preserve task definitions/images/pools/scaling/routing/database settings. There is no saved plan or apply authorization for that proposal.
