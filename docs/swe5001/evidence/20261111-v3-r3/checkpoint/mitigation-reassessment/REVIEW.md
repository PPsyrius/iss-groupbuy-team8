## Evidence boundary

The completed warm case failed **2,961/25,984 requests (11.3955%)** at 600 RPS. Its first ten seconds retained all 1,000 original socket inodes, with **zero additional namespace opens and zero SYN retransmits**, while native gateway/AWS/non-AWS controls failed. Cold 100-VU/600-RPS achieved approximately 595 RPS/37 Mbps cleanly; 1,000-VU/60-RPS was clean. Thus neither initial SYN bursts, held-socket count alone nor aggregate Mbps alone explains the observations. High activity across a large established pool is the lead interaction, not an identified component. Warm-case CPU/cgroup headroom was not measured.

**H1000/A100** below means the sole already-delegated case: 1,000 actual retained sockets, only 100 carrying the high-rate application workload, approximately 600 RPS. Its live files/progress were not inspected. Predictions require verified retention/activity and comparable achieved RPS/bytes; they are conditional, not its result. Even the held sockets can carry transport maintenance traffic.
