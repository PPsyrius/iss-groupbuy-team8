New tests: 100 `BlocRow`s mount with one `setInterval(…, 1000)` that is cleared on unmount; over 120 s a final-hour row commits 120 times, a row 5 h out 2 times, and ended, filled and closed rows 0 times; `clockReading` gives the same countdown, ended test and "Ends today" label as the real time at every second from 3 h out to 5 min past the deadline, at exactly 1 h, 1 day and 0, across Singapore midnight, and for deadlines on and off a whole second.
