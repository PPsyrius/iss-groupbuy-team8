### 3.3.5 UC4 Join a Team

A signed-in buyer joins an open team by paying the team's own price first; once the payment is verified, BlocBuy admits the buyer if the team still has a seat, and the admission that fills the last seat makes the team succeed. The payment steps are the included use case "Pay upfront via Stripe Checkout" (section 3.3.11), and the steps UC4 shares with UC3 are not repeated here.

**Actors:** Buyer (primary; acts as a joining member). Stripe (secondary; through the included use case).

**Preconditions:**

- The buyer is signed in (UC2) and the account is active.
- BlocBuy accepts online payment.
- The team is open, its deadline has not passed, it has a free seat, and the buyer neither leads it nor already belongs to it.

**Trigger:** The buyer presses "Join this bloc" on the listing page, either on the open team's row or on the invite banner shown to a buyer who arrived through a member's invite link (UC7).

**Normal flow:**

1. If the buyer arrived through an invite link, the system resolves the link's reference to the member who shared it («extend» UC7 at the extension point *admission*).
2. The system checks that this buyer may still join it: the team is open and inside its deadline, a seat is free, and the buyer is not already a member. No seat is reserved.
3. The system freezes the buyer's purchase terms as a purchase intent: the team's own price, currency, target size and deadline, plus the sharer from step 1 if any. The intent stays payable for 40 minutes or until the team's deadline, whichever comes first.
4. The buyer pays the frozen amount: «include» Pay upfront via Stripe Checkout (section 3.3.11). The included use case ends with a verified, succeeded payment for this intent.
5. When it records the payment, the system checks the intent, the buyer and the listing again, as in UC3 step 5.
6. The system checks the admission rules against the team as it is now, in this order: the amount paid equals the team's price; the buyer is not already a member; the team is still open and inside its deadline; a seat is free.
7. The system records the membership (with the sharer, if any), increases the team's member count by one and records the outcome of this payment as fulfilled. If this admission fills the last seat, the team succeeds (UC5, success).
8. The system creates the buyer's order with status *pending* and takes one unit of stock. If the team succeeded in step 7, all of the team's pending orders, including this one, become *confirmed* at the same time.
9. The buyer's receipt page shows "Payment received"; the account page shows the team with its seats filled, for example "2 of 2 joined".

**Exceptional flows:**

- 1a. The invite reference is unknown, belongs to another team, or names the buyer themself: it is ignored, and the join continues without attribution.
- 2a. The buyer sends join requests for this team too often in a short time: the system refuses the request and asks the buyer to try again later.
- 2b. The team does not exist, has closed or expired, is full, or already has this buyer as leader or member: the request is rejected before any payment exists. The listing page reloads the team's row so that the buyer sees why.
- 4a. The buyer abandons the payment, or the hosted payment page expires: no membership or order exists (section 3.3.11, 4a).
- 5a. The intent has lapsed, the buyer is inactive, or the listing is no longer purchasable: as in UC3, 5a, the payment is recorded as *refund required* and refunded, and no membership or order is created.
- 6a. The amount paid differs from the team's price, the buyer already joined the team with an earlier payment, or the team closed, succeeded or expired after step 2, including a team that another admission filled: the system refuses the admission. It records the payment's outcome as *refund required* and a refund obligation, creates no membership or order, and the money is returned as in UC6 (section 3.3.7). The receipt shows "Refund pending" until the refund is sent.
- 6b. Two buyers pay for the last seat at the same time: the system admits them one at a time. The first is admitted (step 7) and its admission makes the team succeed; the second meets a team that is no longer open and is refused as closed (6a).
- 6c. Stripe reports the same payment again: the recorded outcome is returned and nothing is created twice.
- 7a. A technical failure happens part-way through steps 5 to 8: nothing of the admission is kept, and Stripe's next report of the payment retries it.

**Postconditions:**

- Success: the team's paid member count has grown by exactly one and never beyond its target size; at most one admission completes the team; the buyer has one paid membership and one order, and the payment has exactly one recorded outcome.
- Refusal: no membership or order exists, only a *refund required* outcome and one refund obligation.

**Analysis objects:** listing page (`JoinTeamUI`) and the payment provider (`PaymentGateway`) «boundary»; `JoinTeamControl` «control»; team, membership, payment, order and refund «entity». Section 3.4.1 models them.
