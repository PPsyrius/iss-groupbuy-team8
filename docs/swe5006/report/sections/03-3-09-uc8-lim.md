### 3.3.9 UC8 View My Orders and Teams

A signed-in buyer opens their account page to see every team they lead or joined, with its current state, and their orders with each order's status. BlocBuy shows both on one page as two lists, each limited to the signed-in buyer. The use case only reads.

**Actors:** Buyer (primary; signed in).

**Preconditions:** The buyer is signed in (UC2).

**Trigger:** The buyer opens "My blocs" (the account page), or returns to it after a payment.

**Normal flow:**

1. The system identifies the buyer from their sign-in, never from anything the page sends.
2. The system reads the buyer's teams, newest membership first: for each, the listing's current title and image, the buyer's role (leader or member), the members joined out of the target, the deadline and the team's stored state.
3. The system reads one page of the buyer's orders, newest first: for each, the product title and image as they were when the order was created, the amount and the order status.
4. The page shows the teams under three filter chips with counts, Active, Completed and Refunded (the last holds every closed team, whatever its refund status), and labels each team "Filling", "Closing soon", "Ended", "Filled" or "Closed". Below them, under "My orders", it shows the orders with their status: "Waiting for the bloc" (pending), "Confirmed" or "Cancelled".
5. The buyer may switch the team filter (without a new request), page through the orders, open a team's listing, or share an open team (UC7).

**Exceptional flows:**

- 1a. The visitor is not signed in: the page redirects to the sign-in page and returns here afterwards (UC2). A request without a sign-in is refused.
- 2a. The buyer has neither teams nor orders: the page shows one empty card that explains how a team works and links to the catalog.
- 2b. A listing behind a team has been removed: the team row stays, without title or image.
- 2c. A team's deadline has passed but the expiry run has not reached it yet: the stored state is still *open*, so the row is labelled "Ended" rather than "Closed" (UC5, expiry 1a).
- 3a. The order list is empty while the team list is not: the page shows a "No orders yet" card under the teams.
- 3b. The requested order page is past the last page: the page moves to the last page instead of showing an empty list.
- 3c. The buyer asks for an order that belongs to someone else: the system answers "not found", the same answer as for an order that does not exist.
- 4a. Either list fails to load: the page shows an error alert with "Try again".

**Postconditions:** The buyer has seen only their own teams and orders. Each team's state agrees with its orders: a succeeded team has confirmed orders and an expired team has cancelled orders. Refund status is not shown here: the "Refunded" filter is named for the refund every closed team is owed and collects all closed teams, including those whose refund is still pending or under review, and each closed row is labelled "Closed", never "Refunded"; only the payment receipt (section 3.3.11) reports whether a refund has been sent. No data has changed.

**Analysis objects:** account page «boundary»; my-activity control «control»; team, membership, order and listing (display fields only) «entity».
