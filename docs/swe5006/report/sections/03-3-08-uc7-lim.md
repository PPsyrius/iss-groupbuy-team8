### 3.3.8 UC7 Share Attribution

A member of an open team shares an invite link; when another buyer joins the team through that link, BlocBuy records who shared it and, after the join has committed, records one share credit for the sharer. UC7 extends UC4 at the extension point *admission* under the condition {via invite link}: a join without a link is unchanged, and attribution can never block or undo a paid join.

**Actors:** Buyer as sharer (primary; the leader or any paid member of the team). Buyer as invited joiner (primary for steps 4 to 8; may be signed out when the link is opened).

**Preconditions:** The sharer is signed in and is a paid member of a team that is open and inside its deadline.

**Trigger:** The sharer chooses to share the team, from the share block on the listing page or from the team's row on their account page.

**Normal flow:**

1. The system checks that the sharer has made fewer than 60 share requests in the last minute, that the team exists, that the sharer is a member of it, and that it is open and inside its deadline.
2. The system returns the sharer's invite reference for this team. On first use it creates one, a random 128-bit reference stored with the team and the sharer; every later request returns the same reference.
3. The page builds the invite link (the listing's address with the team and the reference) and copies it or hands it to the device's share sheet. The sharer sends it outside BlocBuy.
4. The invited buyer opens the link. The page keeps the team and reference in the browser session and removes them from the address bar, so a copied address never re-shares someone else's reference.
5. The system resolves the reference to the sharer's public nickname, without requiring sign-in. The listing page shows an invite banner that names the sharer and offers "Join this bloc".
6. The invited buyer joins the team (UC4), signing in first if needed (UC2). The join request carries the reference. Before it locks the team, the system checks that the reference names this team and a sharer other than the joiner, and it then freezes the sharer on the purchase intent (UC4, steps 1 to 3).
7. When the payment is admitted (UC4, step 7), the system records the sharer on the new membership and announces that a member joined.
8. After the admission transaction has committed, the system records one share credit for the sharer in a separate transaction.

**Exceptional flows:**

- 1a. The team does not exist, or the requester is not a member of it: the system answers "not found" in both cases, so a non-member learns nothing about the team.
- 1b. The team has closed, filled or passed its deadline: the system refuses with `DEAL_CLOSED` and mints no reference.
- 1c. The sharer has made 60 share requests within one minute: the system refuses with HTTP 429 (`RATE_LIMITED`).
- 4a. The link's team or reference is malformed: the page drops it without keeping it, and an older kept link is discarded too (the newest link wins).
- 5a. The reference is unknown, or more than 60 resolutions came from the same network address within one minute: the system answers "not found" or HTTP 429, and the banner shows a generic invitation without a name. The join itself still works, without attribution. A slow resolution also falls back to the generic invitation.
- 6a. The reference names another team, or the joiner is the sharer: it is ignored and the join goes ahead unattributed (UC4, 1a).
- 6b. The invited buyer starts their own team instead of joining (UC3): the reference is ignored.
- 7a. The admission is refused (UC4, 6a), or the same payment is reported again: no member is announced and no credit is recorded.
- 8a. Recording the credit fails: the failure is logged and does not affect the committed join. The sharer recorded on the membership is the source from which a missing credit can be reconciled later.
- 8b. The same join is announced twice: the credit's unique key on the participation absorbs the duplicate.

**Postconditions:** Every attributed join has its sharer on the membership, and each accepted participation has at most one share credit. A rejected or unattributed join has no credit. Credits are recorded but not shown to buyers: a view of a sharer's credits was planned, then closed as not planned in Sprint 4 because it lay outside both courses' grading criteria (Section 1.4.2).

On 24 October the system demo recording on dev showed the invite banner naming the sharer and the invited buyer's paid join (UC4). Credit recording was tested locally (Testcontainers), not read on dev: an invited paid join leaves one credit row, a replay keeps it single, and an unknown reference credits nobody (`StripeLocalIntegrationTest`).

**Analysis objects:** `ShareInviteUI` (the share block, steps 1 to 3) and `InviteLandingUI` (the invite banner, steps 4 and 5) «boundary»; `InviteControl` (minting and resolving the reference), `ReferralControl` (attributing the join, steps 6 and 7) and `CreditControl` (step 8) «control»; team, membership, invite and share credit «entity». Section 3.4.4 models them.
