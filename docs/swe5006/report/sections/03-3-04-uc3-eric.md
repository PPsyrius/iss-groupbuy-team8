### 3.3.4 UC3 Initiate a Team

A signed-in buyer starts a new team for a listing by paying the team price first; once the payment is verified, BlocBuy opens the team with the buyer as its leader and first paid member and records the buyer's order. The payment steps are the included use case "Pay upfront via Stripe Checkout" (Section 3.3.11).

**Actors:** Buyer (primary; acts as the team's leader). Stripe (secondary; through the included use case).

**Preconditions:**

- The buyer is signed in (UC2) and the account is active.
- Online payment is enabled in the environment.
- The listing is on sale, in stock and priced in Singapore dollars, and an admin has set its team terms (UC9): a target size of at least two buyers and a team lifetime between 2,400 and 86,400 seconds (40 minutes to 24 hours).
- A team price is in force for the listing for at least the whole 40-minute purchase window.

**Trigger:** On the listing page, the buyer presses "Start a bloc".

**Normal flow:**

1. The system checks that the listing can start a team and issues a price quote that belongs to this buyer: the team price and the time until which the quote is valid. The quote is stored unchanged and is valid for at most 15 minutes.
2. The listing page shows the quote with a countdown; the buyer chooses to continue to payment.
3. The system freezes the buyer's purchase terms as a purchase intent. Price, currency, target size and both deadlines come from the server and the quote, never from the request. The system reserves an identity for the team that does not exist yet. The intent stays payable for the 40-minute purchase window.
4. The buyer pays the frozen amount: «include» Pay upfront via Stripe Checkout (Section 3.3.11). The included use case ends with a verified, succeeded payment for this intent.
5. In the transaction that records the payment, the system checks the terms, the buyer and the listing again: the intent is unused and not expired, the buyer is still active, and the listing is still purchasable.
6. The system creates the open team, with the buyer as its leader and first paid member, and records the outcome of this payment as fulfilled.
7. In the same transaction, it creates the buyer's order with status *pending* and takes one unit of stock.
8. The buyer's receipt page shows "Payment received" with the order and team references. In the account page the team appears as active with one member, and the listing page offers the leader a share link (UC7).

**Exceptional flows:**

- 1a. The listing has no team terms, is not on sale, has no stock, or has no team price valid for the purchase window: the system issues no quote (for example `PRODUCT_NOT_STARTABLE` or `TEAM_PRICE_UNAVAILABLE`); the quote sheet shows the reason, and nothing is created.
- 1b. Online payment is disabled in the environment: the page disables "Start a bloc"; no quote or intent is created.
- 3a. The quote has expired, belongs to another buyer or listing, or no longer undercuts the individual price: the system refuses it (for example `TEAM_PRICE_QUOTE_EXPIRED`) and creates no intent. The sheet stays open with "Get a new price", which returns to step 1.
- 4a. The buyer abandons the payment, or the hosted payment page expires: no team, membership or order exists. The unpaid intent is marked expired later by a background sweep (Section 3.3.11, 4a).
- 5a. The payment succeeds, but the system then refuses it because the intent has lapsed, the buyer is no longer active, or the listing is no longer purchasable: the payment's outcome is recorded as *refund required*, no team, membership or order is created, and a refund obligation is recorded. The refund is sent by the same refund worker as in UC6 (Section 3.3.7, steps 6 to 9), and the receipt shows "Refund pending" until it is sent.
- 5b. Stripe reports the same payment again: the recorded outcome is returned and nothing is created twice. A payment that is reused for different terms is rejected.
- 7a. A concurrent purchase on another team took the last unit of stock between step 5 and step 7: the whole transaction rolls back, and Stripe's redelivery of the payment result repeats step 5, where the listing is now refused as not purchasable (5a).
- 7b. A technical failure happens part-way through steps 5 to 7: nothing of the transaction is kept, and Stripe's redelivery retries it.

**Postconditions:**

- Success: exactly one open team exists with the payer as its leader, one matching paid membership and one pending order; the stock has dropped by one, and the payment has exactly one recorded outcome.
- Refusal: no membership or order exists; only a *refund required* outcome and one refund obligation exist. The use case never claims that money already moved at Stripe can be undone except by a refund.

On 23 October on dev, a buyer started a two-seat team on a test listing with a Stripe Sandbox card: the payment was verified, the team opened with one member and the order was created (the expiry that followed is UC5 and UC6).

**Analysis objects:** listing page with its quote sheet (`StartTeamUI`) and the payment provider (`PaymentGateway`) «boundary»; `StartTeamControl` «control»; product, team, membership, payment, order and refund «entity». Section 3.4.1 models them.
