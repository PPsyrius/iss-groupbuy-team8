### 3.3.2 UC1 Browse and View Listings

A buyer, signed in or not, browses the listings that are on sale and opens one to compare its individual price with its team price and to see the team that is filling for it. The use case only reads: it creates no team, payment, order or stock change.

**Actors:** Buyer (primary; anonymous or signed in).

**Preconditions:** At least one listing is published (on sale). Signing in is not required.

**Trigger:** The buyer opens the BlocBuy home page, changes a filter or search term, or opens a listing.

**Normal flow:**

1. The buyer opens the catalog page.
2. The system selects one page of on-sale listings in a fixed order, optionally narrowed by category.
3. For each listing on the page, the system finds its best open team.
4. For each listing, the system works out the displayed prices with the pricing rule of the listing's pricing mode: the individual price is the listing price, and the team price is the open team's price when such a team exists in the same currency.
5. The system shows each listing as a card with its title, image, individual price, team price where one exists, and the seats filled of its open team.
6. The buyer opens one listing.
7. The system shows the detail page: images, description, stock and number sold, the team price beside the struck-through individual price, and the open team ("Open blocs") with its seats filled and its deadline.
8. From the detail page the buyer may start a team (UC3) or join the open team (UC4); both need a signed-in buyer (UC2).

**Alternative flow, keyword search (UC1 extension):**

- 2a. The buyer enters a keyword of at most 64 characters. The system splits it into words; a listing matches when every word appears in its title, subtitle or description, ignoring case. The matching listings are shown a page at a time, in catalog order, and the flow continues at step 3. The web page does not use the system's optional re-ordering of results by meaning, so buyers always see the catalog order.

**Exceptional flows:**

- 2b. No listing matches the category or keyword: the system returns an empty page and the buyer sees an empty state, not an error.
- 2c. The keyword is too long, or the requested page is out of range: the web page shortens the keyword and corrects the page before it asks; a search that still breaks these bounds is refused and shows no listings.
- 4a. A listing has no open team, or its open team uses another currency than the listing: the system shows the individual price only. It never shows a zero or invented team price.
- 4b. A listing's pricing mode has no pricing rule. This is a configuration fault, not a buyer error: the system reports an error instead of pricing the listing with another rule.
- 6a. The listing does not exist, or exists but is a draft or delisted: the system answers "not found", and the page shows the not-found card. A hidden listing is never revealed.
- 7a. The listing cannot be loaded for a technical reason: the page shows an error card that says nothing was charged and no team was joined, with a retry button.

**Postconditions:** The buyer has seen only on-sale listings, with prices worked out by the system. No data has changed.

**Analysis objects:** catalog page and listing page «boundary»; browse control «control»; listing (with its pricing mode) and team «entity».
